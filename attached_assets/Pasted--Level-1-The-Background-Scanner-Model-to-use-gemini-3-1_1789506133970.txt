### Level 1: The Background Scanner

  • Model to use: gemini-3.1-flash-lite
  • What it does in your app: This is the model that reads the AI's generated answers and decides which words are "concept prerequisites" that need
  to be underlined.
  • Why: This is a high-volume, repetitive data-extraction task. Since 3.1 Flash-Lite costs a fraction of the standard models ($0.25 per 1M input
  tokens), it can run invisibly in the background every single time a message is sent without ballooning your API costs. It is lightning-fast and
  built exactly for this type of endpoint routing.

  ### Level 2: The Core Chatbot

  • Model to use: gemini-3.7-flash
  • What it does in your app: This is your primary conversational engine. When the user types a normal prompt in the chat (e.g., "Summarize chapter
  4" or "What does this PDF say about X?"), this model handles it.
  • Why: As your log noted, 3.7 Flash is the "Efficient Mid-Tier." It is highly capable of reading the PDF context and giving great answers, but it
  guarantees predictable token output. It won't freeze up and burn hundreds of hidden "thinking tokens" to answer a straightforward study question.
  It keeps the chat feeling snappy and cost-effective.

  ### Level 3: The Deep Academic Tutor

  • Model to use: gemini-3.1-pro (or gemini-3.8-flash on "High Effort" mode)
  • What it does in your app: You trigger this model when the user highlights a massive, complex block of text and asks a deeply specific question,
  or when they click an underlined prerequisite word and need a fundamental pedagogical breakdown of the science/math behind it.
  • Why: This is where you actually want the model to slow down and think. 3.1 Pro is specifically designated for "the absolute hardest problems in
  science, research, and deep reasoning." By reserving this expensive, heavy-duty reasoning model exclusively for deep-dives, you guarantee that
  users get incredibly high-quality tutoring exactly when they need it most, without wasting compute on simple tasks.
